Features

Record a website as a source, with virtually no effort.

We make it effortless to record a source so you can spend more time researching.

Save you time and effort

We take any website on the net and convert it to a source citation automatically. Recording sources becomes a joy, rather than a struggle.

RecordSeek automatically creates an MLA format source citation for you, so you get the benefit of an official citation without the frustration.

“RecordSeek was created to save time and make a broken process simple.”— Dovy Paukstys, Creator

Works everywhere

RecordSeek is designed for mobile as well as desktop. With a few simple instructions you can use it on the go.

With the bookmarklet, you can walk into any library and use RecordSeek without installing a thing. Portability is important to us.

FamilySearch & Ancestry

We aim to support the biggest players in the industry. RecordSeek lets you link your sources to FamilySearch or Ancestry, and we’ll gladly expand to others.

Working together we can build a complete solution for users and developers across the world.

Collaborate with others

The whole reason we cite sources is to avoid duplicated effort. With citations, others won’t search for the same records you already found.

With citations, family fiction fades away. We all want to prove our ancestors existed — so why not work together?

“Teamwork is the fuel that allows common people to attain uncommon results.”— Andrew Carnegie
